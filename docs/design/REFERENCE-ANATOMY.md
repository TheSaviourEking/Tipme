# Reference anatomy

## Desktop

A warm neutral canvas holds a portrait-led recipient object and a raised tipping instrument. The pair is visually related without becoming a dashboard grid. Thin borders, light-facing edges, shallow contact shadows, and controlled ambient shadows create depth. Purple is concentrated in selected amounts and the primary action.

## Mobile

Portrait, identity, story, tags, amount selection, message, and action read as one deliberate sequence. It is not a desktop grid stacked mechanically. Financial disclosure remains visible and controls remain at least 44px.

## Details to preserve

- Crisp neutral canvas and strong black type.
- Personal portrait as the anchor.
- Compact identity card with name, handle, role, story, and interests.
- Raised amount keys and a clearly purple selected key.
- Recessed custom amount, message, and quote areas.
- One unmistakable action.
- A warm but restrained confirmed-success moment.

## Details not to copy literally

The board is art direction. Do not copy fictional balances, unsupported monthly tipping, unverified success, unclear fees, inaccessible text sizes, or decorative objects that obscure the real flow.
