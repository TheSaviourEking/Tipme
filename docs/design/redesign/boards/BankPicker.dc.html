A. Search your bank Proposed

The field becomes a search box. Popular banks sit on top as keys you can tap. Typing filters the list, and short names and nicknames work: "gtb", "gtco" and "guaranty" all find Guaranty Trust Bank; "fbn" and "first" find First Bank. Everything else is A to Z, with microfinance and mortgage banks folded under "More banks". A popular key only shows if that bank is in our partner's list.

  • For keyboards and screen readers: the WAI-ARIA combobox pattern. The input has role combobox with aria-expanded, and the list is a listbox. The highlighted bank is announced, along with "4 banks match" as you type. Esc closes the list without changing anything.
  • Search runs in the browser on the list we already load, with no extra calls. Nicknames come from a short list we keep, for example gtb/gtco → Guaranty Trust; fbn → First Bank; uba → United Bank for Africa; fcmb → First City Monument; stanbic → Stanbic IBTC; opay → OPay (Paycom); moniepoint; palmpay; kuda.

Moniepoint leads by transaction value. The big banks are added by name. We match each key to the partner's list by code, so a missing bank is never offered.

B. Account number first Later: needs a check first

The person types their 10 digits first. Nigerian account numbers carry a check digit worked out from the bank's code, so the browser can tell, with no calls, which banks the number could belong to. Those banks go to the top, usually only a few. We never check names across several banks; the name is only checked after the person picks one.

How the check digit works (CBN NUBAN)
A 10-digit number is a 9-digit serial plus 1 check digit. Put the bank's 3-digit CBN code in front of the serial, so you have 12 digits. Multiply them in turn by 3, 7, 3, 3, 7, 3, 3, 7, 3, 3, 7, 3 and add the results. Then the check digit is 10 − (sum mod 10), and a 10 becomes 0.

Run that for every bank code in the list, and keep those whose result matches the number's last digit. On average about 1 in 10 codes passes by chance, so the shortlist is small but can hold a wrong bank or two. That's why the person still picks, and the name check confirms.
Check before building B; we can't confirm these from our code:
  • Are the partner's codes CBN codes? Our test samples use 058 (GTBank), 101 (Providus) and 232 (Sterling), which look like CBN codes. But that comes from our tests, not from the partner's live list. packages/payments/test/bachs-payouts.test.mjs:521-528
  • Fintechs and microfinance banks such as OPay, PalmPay, Moniepoint and Kuda use longer institution codes under the newer CBN standards, and outside sources disagree on how their check digit works. If B can't place them, it must still show them under "Search all banks", never hide them.
  • A wrong shortlist must never block anyone: "Search all banks" is always there.
Needed from our partner: confirmation of which code standard their bank list uses. With that, B is about a day's work on top of A.
