Three pages built in Page Studio: clearly different, all still good

Each person picked their own colours, fonts and blocks. None of them used a preset. The guardrails did the rest: the palettes are derived and contrast-checked, every row reflows on a phone, photos with words on them get a shade, and each page passed the design check before it could be published. The tip block, fee line, Pro badge, report link and footer are the same TipMe parts on all three. Sample people and content. Proposed
