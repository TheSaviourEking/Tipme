Campaign Studio (Pro): a named link for each place you share

“New single”, “Saturday gig”: each campaign is a short link and its own QR code that lead to the same tip page, so the person sees which places bring tips. It builds on ProAdditions §3 (“Where your tips come from”, approved 5 Nov). Privacy: counts only, never who; no visitor identities; nothing new is stored about a supporter. A campaign here is only a tracked link: no goal, target, end date or stated purpose, so it is not the “tip goals and campaigns” you deferred pending our payment partner’s view. Three screens: the list, creating one, and one campaign’s detail. Sample numbers. Proposed

New campaign

That link is free to use.

What a campaign is, and isn’t

  • It is a link to your normal tip page. Supporters see your page exactly as always, with no campaign name.
  • No goal, no target amount, no end date, no stated cause.
  • Stopping a campaign keeps its results; its link then opens your page like your usual link, so printed QR codes never break.
  • A link ending can’t be one of TipMe’s own words or another person’s handle, and is checked like a handle.

What has to be built

  • The short link itself: a campaign table (name, link ending, page) and the route that opens the page.
  • One short value on each tip: which campaign it started from. No personal data, and nothing added to the money tables.

When Pro ends

  • Every campaign link keeps working and opens the page, so nothing printed breaks.
