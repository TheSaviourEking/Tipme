Kept exactly as the page works today

  • Free pages offer ₦2,000, ₦5,000 and ₦10,000, starting on ₦2,000. A Pro page shows its own 1 to 4 amounts and starts on the one closest to ₦2,000.
  • The smallest tip is ₦2,000. There is no largest.
  • The key's label: “Tip ₦2,000”, then “Starting your tip…” while it starts. The supporter pays exactly that; TipMe's share comes out of the recipient's side.
  • “Add to the celebration” appears only when the person has shared a moment.
  • Test copies add “TipMe is in test mode: no real money moves yet.” With the pilot notice switched on, this line is added: “TipMe is in a pilot. Payments are real. Need help? support@usetipme.xyz”. On phones it sits in the Tip bar.
  • Every field, limit, error message and the order of the form are unchanged. Only the materials change: keys, recessed fields, the black Tip key and the paper panel.
Proposed, needs your yes

Two small changes the page doesn't make today

  • Say the minimum while typing. Today, typing ₦1,500 only changes the key to “Tip”; the message appears after pressing it. The proposal is to show the same message under the field as soon as the amount is below ₦2,000.
  • One line under the Tip key instead of two. Today there are two: “Our payment partner handles this. You’ll confirm on its page.” and “No account needed. You pay exactly this amount.” The Tipper's journey board used one: “You pay exactly this. You'll confirm on our partner's page.” If you prefer that, the second line goes.
