The owner’s idea of 11 Nov: “customise everything, like a site builder.” What’s drawn is the agreed constrained studio: curated themes, each pre-checked so nothing unreadable can be chosen, sections you add, remove and reorder, three tested layouts, a live preview on phone and desktop, and Publish, Undo and Discard changes. Owner, 11 Nov: “They should not be able to make bad designs.” So bad designs are impossible by construction: no colour picker, font pairs only, tested layouts only, sections that snap to the grid, limits on everything, no free positioning and no custom code. How editing works

  • Draft and published are two copies. Supporters only ever see the published one; the draft is saved as you go.
  • Undo steps back through this session’s changes. Discard changes throws the whole draft away.
  • Publish never blocks for design reasons: every choice on screen is already tested. It only waits for a section’s required fields (an event’s date, a service’s name).
  • Preview switches between phone and desktop; both are the real page rendering, not a mock.
  • Entry point: My page › “Open Page Studio” (Pro). Today’s settings for photo, name, cover, links and tip amounts stay where they are.

Every palette is tested before it ships: 4.5 to 1 for text and 3 to 1 for large headings, in its light and dark version. Nothing on screen can fail.
