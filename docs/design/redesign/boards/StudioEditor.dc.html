Page Studio (Pro): design your own page, with guardrails that never let it break

Owner, 12 Nov: “They should be able to make custom UI but no bad ones.” So the Studio moves from presets to guardrails: any brand colour, a self-hosted font library, and a block builder on a fixed grid. The system corrects what would break (it derives every text and surface colour from the person’s picks so contrast always passes, reflows every row to one column on phones, adds a shade behind text on photos) and a design check runs on every edit. Anything it can’t correct is listed with a one-click fix, and the page can’t be published until the list is empty. TipMe’s tip block, fees, Pro badge, report link and footer stay locked. 2 · Freedom where it’s safe: any colour, a font library, blocks on a grid

Each panel lets the person choose freely and quietly applies the rule that keeps the page good. When a choice can’t be used the way they hoped, it says why in plain words and what it does instead.

When a colour can’t carry text

Sule, a barber, picks a bright yellow.

Your yellow is too light for text on a light page. We’ll use it for highlights and for buttons with dark text, and a deeper shade of it for links and headings. Your page will still look yellow.

How it works: the colour is turned into a range of lighter and darker shades of the same hue (OKLCH). For each job, the engine picks the nearest shade that passes. If none keeps the hue, it uses a neutral and says so.

The font library

Up to 2 families: one for headings, one for text. All are stored on TipMe’s servers, so no other site sees the visit.

3 · Rules that apply themselves: phones and photos

Every layout works on a phone, by rule

Below 768 px every row becomes one column, in reading order: left column first, then right. The tip block comes straight after the name. Nobody has to design a phone version, and nobody can break one.

Tip block AB CD
Tip block A B C D

Three columns become two on tablets (768–1023 px) and one on phones. Galleries keep 2 photos across on phones. Text sizes clamp between a phone minimum and a desktop maximum.

Photos: crop, focal point, shade

Fades, Wuse 2
  • The person drags one dot (or uses arrow keys) to say what matters. Every shape is cropped around it.
  • Photos smaller than their spot needs are flagged by the check, with a fix.
  • Whenever text sits on a photo, a shade is added under the text, tested against a pure white photo.
  • Photos below the minimum size are refused at upload, in plain words.
