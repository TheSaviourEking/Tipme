What we will not do, on purpose

  • Tell the person tipped that someone came back. Tips keep only an optional display name, up to 60 characters (schema.ts:889); there is no supporter account or email to match. Matching on names would be wrong often and would expose people who chose to stay unnamed.
  • Thank supporters later by email. TipMe keeps no supporter email, so the thank-you has to be on the receipt, at the moment of paying.
  • Compare supporters. The supporter’s recognition celebrates and never compares (recognition.ts:142).
  • Show a note publicly without its writer’s yes. See the pinned note on “Make it yours”.

  • “Thank you, Chidi.” as the receipt heading when a name is given, instead of today’s “Ada got your thanks.”
  • The weekly share nudge: on the dashboard only, or email too (opt-in either way)?
