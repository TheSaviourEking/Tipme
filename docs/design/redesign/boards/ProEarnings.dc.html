Proposed

Free vs Pro here

  • Free: this week (with the change on last week, as the dashboard shows today), this month, all time, and tip counts. Nothing taken away from today.
  • Pro: the 12-week and 12-month chart, any past week, month or year, average tip, best day, change on the previous period, and the notes.
  • The preview is an example picture, never the person’s own numbers blurred: no teasing with their own data.
  • One preview, once per page. No pop-ups, no red dots.
Proposed

Rules for the numbers and notes

  • Only confirmed tips count. “Being confirmed” tips join when confirmed.
  • A period that isn’t over is compared with the same days of the one before (1–8 Nov against 1–8 Oct), never with a whole month.
  • Notes state facts and counts only: no “because”, no advice, no guesses about why. Words such as “AI”, “smart” or “predict” are never used.
  • A note shows only with enough tips behind it: at least 10 in the period, and the best day at least 5 tips and a quarter of them. Otherwise it is left out, not softened.
  • Repeat supporters count only opted-in supporters, and never name them here.
  • Days and weeks use Lagos time; weeks start on Monday, as the weekly report does.
  • When Pro ends this page shows the Free view. Nothing is deleted; it is all counted from the tips.
