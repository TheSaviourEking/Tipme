3. Recommended model Approved by the owner, 5 Nov 2026

Free: identity is never behind Pro
  • Up to 3 links, picked from every platform marked Free above, including TikTok.
  • Facebook stays free. It is the widest reach in Nigeria; charging for it would make pages look less real, and real-looking pages are what make people tip.
  • Each link is checked against that site's own address, as today, and shows the @handle we read from it.
Pro: more, and in their order
  • Up to 8 links, including the Pro-only kinds (own website, Telegram, LinkedIn, Threads, Patreon, Selar, Substack).
  • One featured link as a full-width key above the row ("Watch me live on Twitch").
  • Their own order, by dragging.
  • See which links get pressed: a count per link on the dashboard (new; today we only see where visitors came from).

If Pro lapses, the extra links are kept but hidden, and the first 3 still show. Nothing is deleted.

4. Adult platforms (OnlyFans, Fansly): owner decided 5 Nov 2026, Pro only

The facts as we could find them. This is not legal advice and implies no regulatory approval.

  • Card networks treat adult content as the highest-risk category. Visa's Integrity Risk Program (from April 2023) puts adult content (MCC 5967) in High Integrity Risk Tier 1, with registration, content moderation, age checks and monthly reporting. Mastercard's rules from 15 Oct 2021 require the acquiring bank to certify the seller's controls, age and identity checks for everyone shown, and fast takedowns. (Visa's own document was seen only through a Chile copy and processor summaries; Mastercard's full rule text is not public.)
  • Nigerian processors exclude it. Paystack's "Ineligible businesses" page lists "Obscene material or pornography" and "Certain sexually oriented materials or services" (support.paystack.com, no date shown). We could not find Flutterwave's own list.
  • Our payment partner's terms decide this for TipMe. We can't see them from here. A page that sends supporters to adult content could be read as TipMe taking adult-content payments, even if the tip itself is "just a tip".
  • App stores: Apple's guideline 1.1.4 bars overtly sexual material; a future TipMe app with pages linking straight to adult sites would be at risk in review.
  • Brand: TipMe is "for the people who make a difference". In 2025 TikTok removed about 3.8M Nigerian videos in one quarter, and an explicit-content flood was reported on TikTok Live in Nigeria (headline seen, body not confirmed). A tipping product can attract the same.
  • A plain website link can point anywhere anyway, so whatever we decide needs an acceptable-use rule and a report button (pages already have "Report this page"), not just a list of sites.
Owner decision, 5 Nov 2026: OnlyFans and Fansly links are allowed for Pro users only. The open points below stay true and are tracked: our payment partner's written position, and the acceptable-use section of the Terms. This is the owner's product decision, not legal advice or a regulatory approval.
Recommendation: don't add OnlyFans, Fansly or other adult-platform links. Revisit only once you have (1) our payment partner's written answer on adult creators and (2) a written acceptable-use policy in the Terms, which today only lists "Acceptable use and how accounts can be suspended" as a heading still to write (apps/web/src/app/legal/terms/page.tsx).
