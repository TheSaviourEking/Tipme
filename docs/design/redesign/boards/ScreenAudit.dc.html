Across the app

  • The recovery-email reminder is a generic purple-edged box on top of every dashboard page. The states work turns it into one calm standing warning, shown on Home and Settings, not on every page.
  • Account and tipping states put a small card in the middle of a wide empty canvas on desktop. They need a composed desktop layout, not just a card.
  • Every generic notice (purple edge, pink error, green success) is replaced by the approved states patterns: slip, result on the key, standing warning, lamps.
  • Money on Withdraw, Tips and Tip detail is set in small grey text. Money should be the instrument: DM Serif numerals on paper, as on Home and the weekly report.
  • The dashboard and settings show the full marketing footer. Proposed: a slim footer inside the signed-in app.
  • Phone shots show “Skip to content” visible in the middle of Withdraw and Settings. Check the skip link is hidden until focused (could be a capture effect).
