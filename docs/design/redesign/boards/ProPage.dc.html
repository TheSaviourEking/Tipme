Questions

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Pro stays on until the end of the month you paid for, then your page goes back to Free. No charge after that.

What happens if Pro ends or a payment doesn’t go through?

You get 3 days to pay. After that your page goes back to Free: Pro extras hide, and nothing is deleted. Your designs, links, amounts and settings are kept, and come back if you return to Pro.

Does Pro change what TipMe keeps?

No. TipMe keeps 10% of each tip on both plans, and withdrawals work the same.

Does the Pro badge mean TipMe checked me?

No. Pro is a paid plan. It shows a page is on Pro, not that TipMe checked who runs it.

What does “Coming” mean?

We’re still building it. Pro members get each one when it’s ready, at no extra cost.
