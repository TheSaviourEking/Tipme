Campaign pages, tip goals, thank-yous and statements (Pro)

The owner decided on 13–14 Nov 2026 to build four Pro features. TipMe Pro is ₦5,000 a month plus a 5% tip fee; Free pays 7%. Campaign pages are Pro’s “multiple tipping pages”: each campaign in Campaign Studio can have its own headline, description, cover, tip goal and thank-you, and its link and QR open a page made for it. Tip goals are a simple target with a bar, worded “tips toward…”, never fundraising. Custom thank-yous show on the receipt and go by email to supporters who left one. Monthly statements are a PDF each month, plus CSV or Excel for any range. It is always the same person: one handle, one balance, one set of withdrawals, the same locked tip area.

8. Rules, and what the owner still needs to answer

Proposed

Rules for building

  • A campaign page is the same person: one handle, balance and set of withdrawals. The tip area is never changed by a campaign.
  • Goals say “tips toward”. Never fundraising, donations, a cause, a promise, a deadline, refunds or all-or-nothing.
  • A goal’s progress counts confirmed tips only, before fees. “Goal reached” shows only after the server confirms the tip that crosses the target.
  • Thank-yous: 140 characters, no links, cleaned like today’s line. One email per tip, only to supporters who asked, never to a refunded or failed tip.
  • Statements come from the ledger and must add up: opening + tips − fees − Pro − withdrawals = closing.
  • The payment partner is never named on a page, a receipt, an email or a statement.
  • Pro is checked on the server for every Pro part; a lapse hides and keeps, never deletes.
Question for the owner

Open questions, with defaults in brackets

  1. How many goals can be active at once? [1 on the main page + 1 per campaign]
  2. Do supporters see amounts or a percentage? [the creator chooses; default percentage]
  3. Is the thank-you email sent once per tip, from TipMe’s address with the creator’s name? [yes, once per tip] [yes, “DJ Kemi via TipMe”]
  4. When is the statement made? [on the 1st, for the month before, emailed as a link]
  5. Do custom covers on campaigns count toward Page Studio later? [yes]
  6. The thank-you line is free today. Free pages can already write one line for the receipt. Keep that, and make campaign thank-yous, card styles and the email version Pro? [yes, Free keeps its one line]

Also needed before building: does a page goal also count tips from campaigns with no goal of their own [yes]; does the creator see a supporter’s email [no]; how long “Goal reached” shows [14 days or until a new goal]; goal target limits [₦5,000 to ₦10,000,000].
