Rules

  • Big celebrations only for rare, meaningful moments: the first tip, a tip landing, the handle claimed, going Pro. Never on routine saves.
  • Routine actions (save, copy, download) get a quiet press and a settle, nothing more.
  • Sound is off by default, except what you decided on 5 Nov: a soft sound for a tip arriving in a background tab, with a mute switch.
  • Vibration only on phones that support it, always short (10–60ms), and never for an error that repeats.
  • Reduced motion turns every shake, rise and drift into a plain change. 18 reduced-motion rules already exist in the CSS; the new ones follow them.
  • One mute setting turns off sound and vibration together. Proposed

What exists today

  • Key press: sinks 2px, 150ms (components.css:144).
  • Tipper paid: one pulse while checking, then a settle and the receipt rising (globals.css:3265–3375; status-view.tsx).
  • Recipient: the bell checks every 10s and shows a toast for 8s (notification-bell.tsx).
  • Milestones at 1, 10, 50 and 100 tips, shown in Moments (rewards.ts:17–20; moments.ts:236–243).
  • No sound and no vibration anywhere in the web app today: a search for vibrate, Audio and play() on origin/dev finds none.
