A new chapter in Settings called “Make it yours”, after “My page”. Every sound and motion here follows the phone’s reduce-motion setting and TipMe’s mute switch; nothing plays before the person has touched the page (browsers block it anyway).

My top five, by what people feel against what it costs

Ranked by impact first, then effort. Impact: how often a person meets it and how much it says “we see you”. Effort: rough build size.

1Your thank-you on the receipt

Every supporter reads it, every time. Turns a payment into a reply from the person.

High impactSmall: one field, one line on the receipt
2Choose your tip sound

The moment a tip arrives becomes theirs. Rides on live updates, which you already approved.

High impactMedium: needs live updates and six sounds
3When a tip lands

Hearts, confetti hearts, a glow or nothing. Small, personal, and it respects calm people.

High impactSmall once live updates exist
4Your colour

A Pro page that matches its cover, from the amount keys to the share card.

Medium impactMedium: colour tokens per page
5A pinned note

Public proof that people love your work, with the writer’s consent.

Medium impactMedium: new consent tick box and pinning

Needs your decision

  1. Thank-you line for Free too? I propose yes: it is the warmest thing on the list, and Pro still gets styles and a signature.
  2. Sounds: six new sounds must be commissioned or licensed. The names here are placeholders for the feel I mean.
  3. Pinned notes: adds a new tick box to the tip form so the writer agrees. Without it, no pinning.
  4. Celebration for Free: Hearts or Off only, or all four for everyone?

Ideas I cut, and why

  • Reordering page sections: the page is short and the order is designed around tipping; moving things would mostly break it.
  • A custom share card per page: the proposed per-person card on the Link previews board already uses the person’s cover and name.
  • Custom fonts: hurts reading and page speed, and makes TipMe pages look unrelated.
