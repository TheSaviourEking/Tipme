Withdraw, redesigned

Built from the real screen (apps/web/src/app/dashboard/withdraw/withdraw-view.tsx and lib/withdraw-state.ts on feat/redesign-polish, now with dev’s withdrawals list, bank line and Show more). Money leads as a dark instrument; the three stacked notice boxes become one line on the instrument, a slip or a standing warning; the history becomes a ledger with stamps and step lamps. Every rule stays: the most you can withdraw comes from withdrawableNow(), the minimum is ₦2,000 or your whole balance if it’s less, and a withdrawal can’t be cancelled. Test mode moves from a full notice box to one calm pill in the header. Its sentence stays one tap away (the pill opens it): “No real money moves in test mode. The test bank pays straight away.” Proposed

What changes

  • Money first: “Available” becomes a dark instrument with DM Serif numerals, its state as one lamp line (“Ready to withdraw ₦5,400”) and the bank as a chip with Change. Today it is a plain text line.
  • Three stacked notice boxes go: test mode becomes a header pill, “Ready to withdraw” moves onto the instrument, and the recovery-email reminder leaves Withdraw (it lives on Home and Settings as a standing warning).
  • The empty right column becomes the ledger: amount, stamp (ON ITS WAY, PAID, FAILED), step lamps for open ones, the note and the bank line, then Show more.
  • Below the minimum gets a red edge and a flag at the field before pressing (today the button just disables).
  • Phone: instrument, form, ledger in that order; the fixed tab bar stays.

Rules kept, and copy

  • withdrawableNow() still decides the most that can go now; pending money never counts.
  • Minimum ₦2,000 or the whole balance if it’s less (FEE_POLICY_V1); “Once sent, a withdrawal can’t be cancelled.”
  • “We couldn’t confirm your withdrawal” carries no money claim.
  • All sentences are today’s (withdraw-view.tsx, lib/withdraw-state.ts, payment-mode-copy.ts) except those tagged Proposed: the “Too small.” flag, stamp words, “Back in your balance: ₦X”, the test-mode pill.
  • Users never see the payment provider’s name.

Copy to settle: withdraw-state.ts:39 says “being checked by the payment provider” while the rows say “our payment partner”. Proposed: “Our payment partner is checking it.”
