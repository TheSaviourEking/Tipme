The Pro badge: a subscription mark, never a check

Where it shows: after the handle on the public tip page, and on the dashboard’s page card and Settings Plan. The rule that matters: nobody should read “paid for Pro” as “TipMe checked this person”. So the Pro mark is never a tick, never green, never says “verified”, and never sits on the line where a business shows Verified business (apps/web/src/components/business/business-header.tsx, green, with “TipMe checked this business’s name, address and contact.”). Three options; sample person.
