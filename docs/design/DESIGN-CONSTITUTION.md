# TipMe design constitution

Status: approved visual authority for frontend work. Product, security, accessibility, performance, privacy, and finance requirements remain authoritative in their own domains.

## Thesis

Make appreciation tangible. TipMe is a carefully engineered personal object: trustworthy, tactile, human, and restrained.

## Direction

- **60% Physical Interface:** believable shallow materials, precise controls, crisp edges, and touch response.
- **30% Editorial Interface:** composed typography, portrait-led identity, asymmetry, whitespace, and concise hierarchy.
- **10% Playful Interface:** selective brand motion and a memorable acknowledgement only after verified success.

This is one system, not three themes. The board in `reference/` is the fidelity target once its original asset is present. Until then, use `REFERENCE-ANATOMY.md` and do not claim final parity.

## Invariants

- Locked colors: `#7A32F9`, `#FFFFFF`, `#000000`.
- Purple is a signature material, not background decoration.
- No generic SaaS cards, indiscriminate pills, glassmorphism, excessive glow, decorative blobs, or stock 3D emoji.
- Typography and composition carry personality before decoration.
- Payment clarity always outranks visual effect.
- Success styling appears only for a server-authoritative confirmed outcome.
- Every interaction works with keyboard, touch, screen reader, zoom, and reduced motion.
- Prefer CSS and authored SVG; a runtime visual dependency requires documented evidence.

## Required reading

Frontend work must also read the anatomy, token/material, component, motion, responsive, iconography, and visual-quality documents in this directory. Record new visual rulings in `DECISION-LOG.md` rather than silently inventing variants.
