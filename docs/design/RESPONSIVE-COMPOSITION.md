# Responsive composition

Design deliberately for 320px, 360px, tablet, standard desktop, and wide desktop.

- Desktop pairs recipient identity and tipping instrument with controlled asymmetry.
- Mobile forms one intentional sequence, not a mechanical column stack.
- Content order remains logical without CSS.
- Long names, biographies, tags, large NGN amounts, errors, and disclosures wrap without overlap.
- No essential control depends on hover or a fixed viewport height.
- The journey remains usable at 200% zoom and equivalent narrow reflow.
