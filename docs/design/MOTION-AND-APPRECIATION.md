# Motion and appreciation

Motion communicates pressure, continuity, hierarchy, or confirmed acknowledgement. It never simulates money movement or delays essential content.

The TipMe Appreciation Moment renders for every trusted `paid` result. Recipient name and confirmed amount remain readable. Optional locally remembered recognition copy is separate and never gates the core confirmation.

Loading, incomplete link, not found, offline/stopped, waiting, checking, declined, and cancelled remain visually distinct and never borrow success choreography. Reduced-motion users receive the same final composition immediately.
