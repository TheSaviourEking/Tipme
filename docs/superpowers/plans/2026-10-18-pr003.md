## Review focus

- Keyboard radio/custom transitions and focus after validation/review/edit.
- Custom decimal input: empty, malformed, excessive precision, zero, excessive amount; no floating-point arithmetic.

- Unknown profiles are genuine unavailable routes; mocks remain noindex and metadata excludes drafts.
- Reflow at 320px and zoom, reduced motion and touch target size; accessibility scans on each meaningful state.
