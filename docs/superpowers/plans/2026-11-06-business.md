# TipMe for businesses: implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task by task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking. Each PR is built by one builder on one lane (`.superpowers/program/builder-rules.md`).

**Goal (release 1, "Business MVP"):** a restaurant, bar, salon or hotel gets a verified business account with locations and managers. Staff join by invitation with their own TipMe accounts. A supporter scans one printed location code, sees who is on shift, picks the person who served them, and tips them with today's tip form and payment. After paying they may leave a kind note with one-tap tags. The business sees counts, tags, notes and busy hours, never a supporter's identity, and no naira per person unless that person opts in. TipMe staff verify, suspend and look up businesses.

**Release 2 (approved to build: the owner reported on 6 Nov 2026 that our payment partner and a legal check said yes):** the share agreement (100/0, 90/10, 80/20, 70/30, 50/50; cap 50%), tip the whole team, and table cards that name the server. Release 1's data model is shaped so release 2 only adds tables, columns and ledger lines; release 2 is planned as buildable PRs after release 1's foundation (B0).

**Spec (binding):** `.superpowers/program/specs/2026-11-06-business-design.md` (owner-approved direction, 6 Nov 2026). **Board:** `Business.dc.html` (canvas v106) in the scratchpad `canvas-email/project/`. **Product background:** PRD v1.1 FR-08. Nothing here is legal advice, implies regulatory approval, or changes a D-item in `docs/00-DECISIONS.md`.

**Architecture:** new tables beside today's, keyed to today's ids. A business has locations and managers (users). A staff member is a **membership**: one TipMe page (`profiles.id`) at one location. A tip made through a business page carries `tips.business_member_id` and `tips.business_location_id`, chosen by the server from a membership the supporter's page view named. The server checks it against the quote's page and **ignores** it if it no longer holds, so attribution can never block or redirect a tip. Money is untouched: the tip still credits `tips.profile_id` through today's quote, intent, attempt, webhook and `buildPaymentPosting`. Notes about a person live in `business_notes`, apart from `tip_messages` (which stay private to the person and are "never copied elsewhere", `schema.ts:979`). Every business number shown is computed at read time with a minimum group of 3. The whole feature is behind `BUSINESS_ENABLED` (off by default), so each PR can merge to `dev` alone without showing half a feature.

**Tech stack:** Drizzle/Postgres, NestJS, Next App Router, Zod, Playwright, `node --test`. No new dependency.

## Before writing tasks: a builder's check of the spec

### Questions only the owner can answer (each changes what gets built)

Each has a default the builders ship with if the owner does not answer.

- **Q1. Must a staff member's own TipMe page be live to be listed at a business?** Today a tip needs a live, active page (`intents.ts`, `TIP_NOT_PAYABLE`) and, on a live copy, the person's own payment account (`acceptingTips`). **Default:** yes, today's rules. A person who doesn't want a public page keeps it live with visibility "hidden". The alternative, "tippable only at the business, with no personal page", changes the tip pipeline's payable check and the public page, so it is a separate PR.
- **Q2. Phone invitations.** There is no SMS or WhatsApp sender (D-11: phone codes are deferred). **Default:** "invite by phone" makes a one-time invitation link that the manager sends from their own phone ("Copy link" and "Share on WhatsApp"). It works once, for 7 days, and any signed-in account may accept it. Email invitations must be accepted by an account whose proven email matches. The alternative waits for an SMS provider.
- **Q3. Who can create a business?** **Default:** any signed-in TipMe user applies from their dashboard (business name, first location, address, contact). TipMe staff verify it before the codes go live. The applicant uses their normal account, which has its own personal page; that page may stay not live. The alternative, staff creating businesses on request for the pilot, replaces PR B1's form with an ops form.
- **Q4. When does the business see a note?** **Default:** at once (post-moderation). Text with links, emails or phone numbers is refused when written. A note is hidden from the business as soon as the staff member or a manager reports it, until TipMe staff review it. The alternative holds every note with text until a TipMe staff member approves it. That adds a review queue and needs people to staff it.
- **Q5. Naira at location level.** The spec forbids naira _per person_ unless that person opts in, but is silent on a location's total. **Default:** no naira anywhere in the business view except the weekly amounts of people who chose to share theirs. The alternative adds a location total, shown only when at least 3 people tipped.
- **Q6.** (Withdrawn: the owner approved release 2, including table cards, on 6 Nov 2026. Release 2's own questions are Q7 to Q10, in the release 2 section.)

### Spec items "open for the owner" (defaults ship; listed again at the end)

- O1 Who is listed: on shift first, then everyone (default) vs on shift only.
- O2 Verification evidence: staff check name, address and a contact; no document upload (default).
- O3 Tag list: Friendly, Fast, Went the extra mile, Great advice, Made my day (default).
- O4 Business plan price: later, with billing (nothing built).

### Minor calls I settled (builders follow them; each is cheap to change)

- **M1** The tip form at a business is today's form unchanged. Only the heading reads "{first name} at {business}". _Cost if wrong:_ hiding the message field is one prop.
- **M2** The staff code is per membership: `/b/<business>/<location>/<handle>`. Someone who works at two locations gets two codes. A code for someone who has left, or whose business is suspended, sends a 307 to `/@handle`, their own page, and the tip is not attributed. A renamed handle resolves through `handle_aliases` as `/@` does. _Cost:_ a URL shape.
- **M3** Only people who can take a tip right now are listed: page live and active, and `acceptingTips` true. Nobody taps a name and hits a dead end. _Cost:_ one filter.
- **M4** A shift ends by itself after 14 hours (`SHIFT_MAX_HOURS`), so a forgotten check-out never leaves someone "on shift" for days. _Cost:_ a constant.
- **M5** Every number the business sees that counts tips or notes shows as "Fewer than 3" below 3. That covers per-person counts and tags, hours, tag shares and shared amounts. Notes are shown one by one (they are the point), with **the date only, no time**, so a note can't be matched to a table at 8:14 pm. _Cost:_ the copy and one helper.
- **M6** A person who leaves stays in the location's totals. Their row, their name and their notes disappear from the business view (spec 8: "the business keeps counts only"). Their shared amounts stop. _Cost:_ one query condition.
- **M7** Business and location slugs are chosen at application. They lock once the business is verified, because printed codes are never reprinted. Staff may correct a slug before verifying. _Cost:_ an alias table later if wanted.
- **M8** The phone remembers the last person tipped at a business only when the supporter ticked today's "Remember my tips on this phone". It is stored with the existing `recognition.ts` record, in the browser only. _Cost:_ one condition.
- **M9** A note is written once per tip, with the receipt's status token, within 7 days of payment. It is at most 280 characters (as a tip message) with 0 to 5 tags, and needs at least a tag or some text. It can't be edited. _Cost:_ constants.
- **M10** The verified badge reads "Verified business", with the line "TipMe checked this business's name, address and contact." It never says "approved", "licensed" or "regulated". _Cost:_ copy.
- **M11** A business's location page and staff pages show only once it is verified and not suspended. Before that, and while suspended, they show "This business isn't taking tips on TipMe right now." with a 200, `noindex`. Suspension stops attribution at once. Tips through a person's own page go on as today. _Cost:_ none.
- **M12** Codes can be printed only once the business is verified, so no sticker goes up that leads nowhere.
- **M13** Account deletion ends that person's memberships and shifts. It is refused while the person is the only owner of a verified business (`BUSINESS_LAST_OWNER`, "Make someone else an owner of {business} first, or close it."). That mirrors `HAS_BALANCE`.

business_invitations
  id uuid pk, business_id, location_id uuid null
  kind text not null                   -- 'staff' | 'manager'

  email_lower text null                -- null = a link invitation (Q2)
  code_sha256 text not null unique     -- only the hash, like staff_enrolments
  invited_by uuid not null, created_at, expires_at
  accepted_at, accepted_by uuid null, declined_at, revoked_at   -- at most one set

business_shifts
  id uuid pk, member_id -> business_members.id

Every business table gets an index on its foreign keys used in reads. Nothing here touches `profiles`, so `live-payouts-schema.test.mjs` needs no change. Check that rule again if a builder adds a `profiles` column.

### Why release 2 needs no rework of this

- **"Tips outside the business are never shared"** is simply `tips.business_member_id IS NULL`. The share keys off the membership a tip was attributed to _at intent time_, which is already recorded and immutable.
- **Agreements reference `business_members.id`.** It is stable for the life of a membership and new when someone rejoins, so an old agreement never carries over.
- **The share is a separate ledger line** (R2-A): a new account code `business_payable` on a new nullable `ledger_accounts.business_id`. The person's `recipient_payable` line is the net minus the share. Release 1's postings, accounts and balances are untouched.
- **Refunds reverse both parts** (R2-A): a reversal transaction mirrors every line of the original payment transaction, found by `ledger_transactions.tip_id`. A shared tip therefore reverses the person's part and the business's part in one balanced posting.
- **Team tips split "among people on shift at that time"** (R2-D), read from `business_shifts` timestamps that release 1 already keeps.
- **Table cards** (R2-E) point at a membership and a shift, both of which exist.

## Global Constraints

- **Workspace.** One branch and worktree per PR, off `origin/dev` _after B0 has merged_ (B0 is off `b86c7b99` or later). Builders follow `.superpowers/program/builder-rules.md`: their own lane, their own integration template, no push, no PR. Never bare `git stash`.
- **Read first:** `AGENTS.md`; the scoped `AGENTS.md` files (`packages/contracts`, `apps/api`, `apps/web`, `apps/worker` if touched, `packages/ui`); `docs/design/DESIGN-CONSTITUTION.md` and the documents it requires for any UI; `docs/00-DECISIONS.md`; `docs/agent/ACTIVE_TASK.md` and `HANDOFF.md`, which each PR updates.
- **Money safety (HARD STOP).**
  - Release 1 changes no money movement. Every tip still credits `tips.profile_id` through today's quote, intent, posting and payout code.
  - Attribution is a label the server chose and checked. A bad, stale or foreign `businessMemberId` is **ignored**, never refused, so it can't block a tip. It can never move money elsewhere, because it is not read by any posting.
  - The intent fingerprint appends the _validated_ member id only when present, so every existing fingerprint (in-flight replays included) stays byte-identical.
  - No ledger, payout, fee or quote file changes in release 1, except `intents.ts` (B0) and read-only queries.
  - No client-supplied truth: tip counts, amounts and shift state come only from the database.
- **Privacy.**
  - The business never receives a supporter's name (`payer_display_label`), contact details, tip message (`tip_messages`), status token, exact time of a tip, or a payment reference. Nothing in any `/v1/me/businesses/*` response comes from `tip_messages` or `payer_display_label`. A test proves it (B7).
  - Every number shown to a business goes through `shownCount` (k ≥ 3).
  - Naira per person appear only for a member with `share_amounts = true` and at least 3 tips in the week. Nothing else ever shows naira to a business (Q5).
  - Ops sees business contacts masked: `maskEmail`, phone as the last 4 digits only. The full contact needs `private.view` with a reason, audited (access-control rule 6).
  - Note text is never logged, put in analytics, metadata, OG or JSON-LD. Analytics events carry no business ids beyond the slug on public page views (`apps/web/AGENTS.md`).
  - What the business sees is written into the privacy page and the decisions log before the flag is turned on (B9).
- **The flag.** `BUSINESS_ENABLED` (`apps/api/src/config.ts`, `flag` like `LIVE_UPDATES_ENABLED`, default off). While it is off, every route in the table answers 404 through a guard (`business-enabled.guard.ts`, B0), `business-roles` included. The web shows no Business or Work link and renders `/b/*` as unavailable. Also while it is off, `createPaymentIntent` ignores `businessMemberId` (the API strips it before calling). Integration and e2e tests turn it on.
- **Copy.** Users never see the payment provider's name (`apps/web/test/no-provider-names.test.mjs`); say "our payment partner". There are no legal or regulatory claims (M10). Pronouns are "they". There is no price anywhere, because the business plan is later (O4).
- **UI.** Use the states primitives already on dev (`Slip`, `EmptySlip`, `ResultKey`, `StateLine`, field errors from states PR A). **Never** use `Notice` or `EmptyState`, which states PR I deletes. Every control is at least 44px with a visible label. Colour never stands alone. Radios and tag toggles sit in a `fieldset` with a `legend`. Tag toggles are `aria-pressed` buttons. Errors are tied with `aria-describedby`. `expectAccessible(page)` runs in every new e2e test. The layout is mobile-first: the supporter's flow is drawn for a phone at 360px.

## Review Focus

1. **Attribution can't move or block money** (B0 Task 3). A foreign, left, suspended or stale member id is ignored and the tip proceeds exactly as today. The fingerprint is unchanged for requests without one. The posting has the same lines.
2. **Nothing private reaches a business** (B7 Task 3, B5). There is no supporter name, message, exact time or reference. k ≥ 3 holds on every number. Naira appear only with opt-in.
3. **Invitations can't be abused** (B3). Businesses never create accounts. Email invitations match a proven email. Codes are hashed and single-use, expire in 7 days and are revocable. Unknown, used and wrong-email codes get one answer. A link can't be accepted twice in a race.
4. **Who can do what in a business** (B1, B3, B6, B7). A location manager acts only on their location. Only owners manage managers. Any other business's id answers 404.
5. **QR-sticker fraud** (B4, B8). The verified badge, business name and location are always shown on top. Unverified or suspended businesses show nothing tippable. Slugs lock at verification.
6. **Moderation** (B5). A reported note disappears from the business in the same transaction. Restore or remove is staff-only, audited, and reads the text only through a reveal.
7. **The flag** (B0). With it off, every business route is 404 and the tip pipeline ignores `businessMemberId`.

## Release 2: share agreement, tip the whole team, table cards

**Status.** On 6 Nov 2026 the owner reported that our payment partner and a legal check said yes to the share agreement and team tips, so release 2 is approved to build (spec, "Release 2"). The plan records that report (R2-0 Task 26). It is the owner's report, not a regulatory approval, and no D-item's status changes because of it. The split options are 100/0, 90/10, 80/20, 70/30 and 50/50, with the cap at 50%: staff always keep at least half.

**Flags.** The share and team tips each sit behind their own flag on top of `BUSINESS_ENABLED`: `BUSINESS_SHARES_ENABLED` and `TEAM_TIPS_ENABLED`, both off by default. Table cards ride `BUSINESS_ENABLED`. With a flag off, the API ignores its fields, as B0 does for attribution, and every posting is today's.

**Release 2 money constraints (HARD STOP, in addition to the Global Constraints).**

- The share is its own ledger line: `business_payable` for the business. The person's `recipient_payable` line is their net minus the share. Both lines sit in the one balanced payment transaction.
- The share is fixed **at intent time** on the tip (`tip_business_shares`). It never changes afterwards and never applies to a tip made before both sides agreed.
- A tip without `business_member_id` (through the person's own page) can never get a share row. This is enforced by a database check through a trigger or a `NOT NULL` foreign key to the tip's member, not only in code.
- Every share and every team split is an integer number of kobo. Rounding goes to the person (the share is rounded down). Lines always add up to the gross, and `isBalanced` holds.
- A refund or chargeback reverses **every line** of the original payment transaction, the person's part and the business's part, in one balanced `reversal` transaction keyed by the provider event. Reversals are idempotent and are never retried blindly against the partner, because TipMe only records them: the partner moved the money.
- Concurrency tests: two accepts racing, an accept racing a tip, and a reversal racing a withdrawal.

**Owner questions for release 2 (defaults ship).**

- **Q7. How is a refund or chargeback reversed?** Today nothing in the ledger changes on a refund. It opens an issue (D-05 says OPEN). **Default:** staff confirm each reversal as a serious action (`tip.reverse`) from the existing refund or dispute issue, after checking the partner's dashboard. It is never automatic, because the partner's refund event doesn't say whether a refund is partial. A partial refund reverses each line in proportion, with the remainder kobo on the person's line.
- **Q8. What if the money was already withdrawn?** **Default:** the reversal posts anyway. The payable may go below zero, withdrawals for that person or business stop until it is back above zero (as `HAS_BALANCE`-style refusals), and a money issue opens. Nothing is collected automatically.
- **Q9. A share of what?** **Default:** the share comes from what the person receives after TipMe's fee (today's 10%, ADR-0014), so TipMe's fee and the supporter's total are unchanged. The tip page sentence becomes "Of what Tobi receives, they keep 90% and 10% goes to The Yellow Chilli." The spec's sentence, "Tobi keeps 90% of your tip", would be inaccurate on a net split. If the owner wants the spec's exact sentence, the share must come from the gross, which changes the posting and the fee line.
- **Q10. Do team tips carry the business's share?** **Default:** no. A team tip is split among the people on shift only.

**Minor calls for release 2.**

- **M14** A proposal shows the member the split, the date it starts and "You keep {n}%". Countering picks another option from the list. The business sees only "Accepted", "Pending" or "No change"; a declined proposal reads "No change" (spec: declines hidden).
